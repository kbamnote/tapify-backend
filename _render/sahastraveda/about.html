About us

Tools for a tradition that is still practised

Sahastraveda was founded in 2024 in Nagpur to put dependable instruments in the hands of Ayurvedic practitioners.

Our work

Between the classical texts and a working clinic

Sahastraveda supplies the instruments that Panchakarma and para-surgical practice depend on: Agnikarma shalakas in tamra, rajat and loha, flame guns and refills, Basti moulds and kits, Swedana yantras, Shirodhara patras, Dhoompan sets, Raktamokshan cupping sets and the disposables a clinic gets through every week. We were founded in 2024 and work out of Vedic Genesis, Shree Ram Nagar, Nagpur. The catalogue is deliberately narrow: instead of listing everything, we stock the pieces practitioners actually reach for, in the metals and sizes the procedures call for. Most of what we send out goes to working clinics and to practitioners setting one up, which is why so much of the range is sold as complete sets. If you are equipping a room from scratch, tell us which therapies you run and we will build the list with you.
